Built on Trust & Reliability

Global Freight Lines Inc is a Chicago-based trucking company focused on delivering more than just freight. We prioritize safety, clear communication, and treating every load as if it were our own. Operating since 2012 with active MC-785359 and USDOT 1309626 authority.

Doing business since 2012 directly with manufacturers, industrial and retail suppliers, freight brokers and forwarders, and 3PLs.

Our team of professional drivers and dispatchers works around the clock to keep your supply chain moving. All drivers hold current Hazmat certification, TSA clearance, TWIC cards, and tanker endorsements. From single pallets to full truckloads, we have the capacity and the commitment to get the job done right.

Global Freight Lines Inc is a DOT licensed Hazmat carrier. We are authorized to access all major Chicago rail yards and ports, and hold signed agreements with all major ocean container shippers and equipment owners.

Strategically located near O’Hare International Airport (ORD) in Bensenville, Illinois, our terminal provides fast and easy access to all major highways for efficient local, regional, and long-haul service.
